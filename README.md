# Coke Sales Analysis Using Excel

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 2021 sales of the Coca-Cola beverage portfolio across U.S. retailers. It examines sales, units sold, operating profit and operating margin to identify strong markets and opportunities for improvement.

## Business Objective
How can Coca-Cola optimize sales and profitability across U.S. retailers?

## Dataset
The supplied educational dataset contains 3,888 records covering:
- Four retailers
- Five regions and 50 states
- Six beverage brands
- January to December 2021

Fields include retailer, invoice date, region, state, city, beverage brand, price per unit, units sold, total sales, operating profit and operating margin.

## Tools and Methods
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel tables and formulas
- SUMIFS for grouped comparisons
- MONTH for monthly analysis
- PivotTables
- Charts and conditional-formatting heatmaps
- Dashboard dropdown filters

## Data Preparation
- Excluded title rows, blank surrounding columns and unrelated markers.
- Organized the analytical data into an Excel table.
- Checked date and numeric formats.
- Checked missing values and exact duplicate records; none were found in the analytical fields.
- Verified sales against price multiplied by units.
- Verified operating profit against sales multiplied by operating margin.

## Key Results
| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total sales | $8,684,027.50 |
| Units sold | 17,148,250 |
| Operating profit | $3,173,631.88 |
| Overall operating margin | 36.55% |

- Sodapop generated the highest retailer sales, approximately $4.40 million.
- West generated the highest regional sales, approximately $2.84 million.
- BevCo achieved the highest retailer operating margin, approximately 41.97%.
- December recorded the highest sales.
- July recorded the highest units sold and operating profit.

Overall operating margin is calculated as total operating profit divided by total sales. Average realized selling price is total sales divided by total units sold.

## Recommendations
1. Plan inventory and replenishment capacity for the July and December peaks.
2. Maintain strong account support for Sodapop.
3. Investigate product mix, discounting and costs behind lower margins in West and FizzySip.
4. Test improvements in comparable locations before expanding them.

## How to Use
Download the Excel workbook and open it in Microsoft Excel. Begin with Dashboard, use the Region and Brand dropdown filters, and review Comparisons and Geography for supporting analysis. Preparation explains the methodology, and Demo Script provides presentation guidance.

## Limitations
The dataset covers only one year, so recurring seasonality cannot be confirmed. Pricing comparisons show associations rather than causal effects. Store counts, promotion spending, inventory availability and detailed cost data are not provided.

Results describe the supplied project dataset, not Coca-Cola’s audited company-wide financial performance.
